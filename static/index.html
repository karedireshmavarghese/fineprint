    Where claims lose money

    Room costs more than your cap
    Insurers shrink surgeon, nursing and theatre charges in the same ratio, not just the room line.
    Items on the non-payable list
    Gloves, admission kits and similar items are billed to you even on a cashless claim.
    The same charge twice
    A repeated scan or test is easy to miss on a long bill. Get it removed before you pay.
    Missing or mismatched papers
    A discharge summary that disagrees with the bill can hold up the whole claim.

    Three steps at the discharge counter

    1. Photograph the bill and discharge summaryYour phone camera is enough. Handwritten lines are read too.
    2. Add your policyUpload the PDF. We pull out the room cap, sub-limits and exclusions.
    3. Get your marked-up copyEach cut shows the clause behind it and what to ask the hospital to change.

    What Fineprint is not

    It gives an estimate, not a decision. Your insurer has the final say. Fineprint doesn’t file claims, doesn’t give legal advice, and tells you plainly when it can’t read a line.
